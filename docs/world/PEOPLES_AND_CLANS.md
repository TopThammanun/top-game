# Peoples And Playable Clans

Implements steps 21-30 of `docs/TBH_GAME_SPEC_1_TO_100.md`.

## Playable People

**Felikin** are the primary playable people of Whisker Realm. The player's party is built from Felikin cat knights, menders, casters, scouts, and support weavers.

Other peoples should enrich the world rather than replace the Felikin fantasy:

- **Woolfolk:** crafters, merchants, loom keepers, upgrade NPCs.
- **Houndsworn:** honorable rivals, allies, challenge NPCs, guild contacts.
- **Tinkerkin:** base builders, device makers, automation unlocks.
- **Mothkin:** Silkspiredale mystics, lore keepers, magic trainers.
- **Ratterlings:** scouts, smugglers, information brokers, trickster enemies or allies.

## Starter Party

The first playable party should teach the full role model:

- **Ironmane Guardian:** tank/front.
- **Wildstripe Striker:** DPS/middle.
- **Silkpaw Weaver:** support/rear.
- **Cloudfur Mender:** healer/rear.

Shorthold/Emberpaw should appear early as the friendly tutorial companion or first alternate unit. Siamheart/Voicecasters should be introduced as the magic identity in Silkspiredale.

## Display Identity Rule

Every character must have three readable identity layers:

- **Clan:** silhouette, culture, home region, and sound flavor.
- **Combat role:** tank, DPS, support, healer, or balanced starter.
- **Cute hook:** collar, hat, ribbon, pose, voice, animation, or personality detail.

If a character cannot be described with all three layers, it is not ready for production.
