# Story And Regions

This file implements steps 12-19 of `docs/TBH_GAME_SPEC_1_TO_100.md`.

## Main Story Goal

The player is the **Threadbound**, a guide whose bond with Yarnthread lets them gather a party of four cat knights and restore Purralia region by region.

The main campaign goal is:

> Reweave Purralia before The Unravel loosens the Yarnthread that holds the Great Weave together.

## Campaign Spine

The campaign follows a restoration arc:

- Discover the first frayed thread.
- Form the starter party.
- Protect Emberhearth and The Great Hearth.
- Learn Threadweaving in Silkspiredale.
- Restore supply routes through Tuna Coast.
- Survive the muted cold of Frostmane Reach.
- Enter The Unwoven Wastes to face the source of the current Unraveling.

## Act 1 - Emberhearth / Pawmont

**Purpose:** tutorial region, first hub, party formation, and emotional anchor.

**Core fantasy:** warm stone halls, candlelit markets, soft banners, hearth smoke, beginner knights learning to protect home.

**Key location:** **The Great Hearth**, a massive fire said to warm the Yarnthread beneath Pawmont. It never goes out until the first signs of The Unravel cause it to flicker.

**Gameplay unlocks:**

- starter party of four
- basic auto-battle
- taskbar skill commands
- AFK reward chest
- first equipment drops

**Act conflict:** frayed threads appear along roads near Pawmont, causing harmless places to become slightly wrong: signs point nowhere, market stalls repeat the same sound, and small knot-like monsters gather near warm light.

## Act 2 - Silkspiredale

**Purpose:** introduce Threadweaving, magic affinities, caster/support identity, and deeper lore.

**Core fantasy:** silk towers, misty bridges, bells in the fog, scholarly magic, soft glowing threads.

**Key location:** **The Singing Loomtower**, where magic threads hum in tune when the Great Weave is healthy.

**Gameplay unlocks:**

- Threadweaving schools
- Rune/Talent Tree
- caster and support spotlight
- first affinity counterplay

**Act conflict:** The Unravel introduces discord into the Loomtower. Spells still work, but their sounds drift out of tune and create unstable thread knots.

## Act 3 - Mewrrowmarsh / Tuna Coast

**Purpose:** introduce economy, crafting resources, merchants, fishing-flavored reward cadence, and equipment upgrades.

**Core fantasy:** seaside stalls, rain on lanterns, fish crates, cozy inns, marsh paths, boats tied with bright thread.

**Key location:** **The Lantern Pier**, the trade route that keeps Purralia's soft economy moving.

**Gameplay unlocks:**

- craft materials
- merchant NPCs
- equipment upgrade/reroll
- resource routes
- event reward structure

**Act conflict:** supply lines tangle. Cargo arrives knotted together, maps become unreadable, and trade NPCs ask the Threadbound to clear frayed route nodes.

## Act 4 - Frostmane Reach

**Purpose:** introduce healer mastery, debuff cleansing, defense challenges, and cozy winter contrast.

**Core fantasy:** cold outside, warm inside; snowbound shrines, thick blankets, quiet bells, steam rising from soup.

**Key location:** **The Snow-Warmed Shrine**, a Cloudfur sanctuary that teaches healing as a form of reweaving.

**Gameplay unlocks:**

- healer spotlight
- cleanse mechanics
- shield/defense encounters
- survival waves

**Act conflict:** Hushweave scars dull healing and slow the party. The player must keep warmth and sound alive in places where The Unravel is trying to make the world silent.

## Act 5 - The Unwoven Wastes

**Purpose:** late-game region, end-game waves, prestige hooks, and direct confrontation with The Unravel.

**Core fantasy:** pale ground, floating loose thread, half-formed ruins, distant purr-like echoes that feel almost forgotten.

**Key location:** **The Broken Spindle**, one of the old supports of the Great Weave.

**Gameplay unlocks:**

- end-game wave sets
- high-risk affix farming
- Reweave/prestige hooks
- advanced party optimization

**Act conflict:** The Unravel no longer hides. It pulls Yarnthread from the world openly, forcing the Threadbound to restore regions under pressure.
