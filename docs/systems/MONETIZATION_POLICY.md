# Collection, Gacha And Monetization

Implements steps 71-80 of `docs/TBH_GAME_SPEC_1_TO_100.md`.

## Monetization Promise

TBH must remain cozy, friendly, and clearable without spending.

Money can buy:

- cosmetics
- convenience
- collection acceleration
- premium battle pass value
- optional extra pulls

Money must not buy:

- exclusive story completion
- direct unavoidable victory
- hidden pity manipulation
- pressure through fake timers
- relief from intentionally painful progression

## Gacha Rules

- Display rates.
- Use visible pity.
- Provide shard/event/exchange paths.
- Handle duplicates fairly.
- Avoid limited banners that create panic.

## F2P Rule

The main story must be clearable without payment. Spending can shorten collection time but cannot be required to finish Purralia's main restoration arc.

## Battle Pass

Battle Pass must include:

- free track with meaningful rewards
- premium track with strong value but no mandatory power
- cosmetics and currency as the main premium appeal
- daily/weekly task fit without heavy grind

## Cosmetic Shop

Sell:

- collars
- hats
- fur colors or patterns
- skill effects
- profile flair
- base decorations

## Rewarded Ads

If included, rewarded ads are optional and diegetic: a cat or NPC offers them politely. They must never interrupt combat, chest opening, onboarding, or story.

## Store Opening Rule

Open shop layers gradually:

- early: no store pressure
- after player understands upgrades: starter-friendly bundles
- after collection unlocks: gacha/cosmetics
- after daily loop: battle pass

## Monetization Check

Every paid feature must answer yes:

> Is this still cozy, fair, and free-to-finish?
