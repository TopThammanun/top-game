# Sound, Music And Game Feel

Implements steps 81-90 of `docs/TBH_GAME_SPEC_1_TO_100.md`.

## Prototype Rule

Sound design starts during prototype. Do not wait until content is complete.

## Music Direction

Use cozy medieval fantasy instrumentation:

- lute
- harp
- recorder
- frame drum
- piano
- nylon guitar

Music should support long idle sessions through adaptive layering.

## Adaptive Music Layers

Starter layer model:

- ambient bed
- warm chord layer
- melody layer
- soft percussion layer
- tension layer for boss/wave pressure

Layers fade in and out based on region, wave pressure, boss state, and chest/reward moments.

## SFX Principles

SFX must be:

- tight
- tactile
- short
- varied
- non-fatiguing

High-priority cues:

- click
- reward
- loot
- level up
- chest open
- skill ready
- boss telegraph
- wave clear

## Cat Voice

Use short syllables:

- mya
- nya
- mrr
- prr

Pitch and rhythm should vary by character size, role, and emotion.

## Purr Ambient

Purr ambience is the game's signature soft layer. It must be subtle and controllable in settings.

## Reward Audio Layers

Use 3-5 intensity levels:

- tiny drop
- rare drop
- level up
- chest open
- boss clear

## Settings

Audio settings must include:

- master
- music
- SFX
- voice
- ambient

## Benchmark

Opening chests, collecting loot, and leveling up must feel good to hear repeatedly.
