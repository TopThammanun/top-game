# localization — Thai + English, extensible

The design corpus is authored in Thai (`project_main_setting.md`) but the
production title and identity vocabulary is English. Ship bilingual from day
one; a monolingual game that adds a second language later grows tech debt
faster than any other localisation-adjacent decision.
